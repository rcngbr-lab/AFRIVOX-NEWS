# AFRIVOX NEWS staff training guide

**Purpose:** qualify staff for the exact AFRIVOX NEWS role assigned to them through supervised staging exercises.  
**Current boundary:** training may use fictional data only. Production, real source identities, live subscriber data, real billing and physical print remain unauthorized.

## 1. Training ownership and evidence

The Editor-in-Chief owns editorial qualification; technical operations owns account/security training; the DPO/privacy owner, legal reviewer, multimedia lead, commercial owner and finance owner approve their subject modules. These are responsibilities to appoint—not evidence that appointments already exist.

For every learner retain: role, release/environment, instructor, exercise data set, expected/actual result, failed attempts, remediation, completion date and signatures. Do not store passwords, MFA seeds or real confidential content in training records.

## 2. Mandatory foundation for every staff member

### Module A — account and security (45 minutes)

Learning outcomes:

- identify the authorized URL and phishing indicators;
- use an individual password and MFA/recovery procedure;
- log out and understand session expiry/revocation;
- recognize source, audience, media, commercial and finance data classifications;
- report a lost device, suspicious login or accidental disclosure without destroying evidence.

Exercise A1: sign in to staging, complete any forced password/MFA step, log out, and confirm the protected page redirects to login.  
Exercise A2: attempt one action outside the learner’s role and record the expected denial.  
Pass condition: both work without credential sharing or privilege escalation.

### Module B — editorial independence and safety (60 minutes)

Cover the editorial charter, corrections, public/private source separation, right of reply, conflicts, vulnerable people, AI limits, sponsored-content separation and escalation. The learner must explain why system administrator, sales and finance authority do not grant publication authority.

Exercise B1: classify fictional public, internal, confidential and restricted material.  
Exercise B2: identify a fabricated quotation, an unverified image and a commercial request that must be rejected/escalated.

## 3. Role learning paths

### Reporter / contributor path

1. Submit a fictional pitch without source identities.
2. Respond to a clarification request.
3. Start an assignment and create a linked draft.
4. Put public references and encrypted internal evidence in the correct fields.
5. Upload a fictional licensed image with complete rights metadata.
6. Submit for review, respond to revision and mark the assignment complete.
7. Draft a live update and verify it remains non-public until independent approval.

Pass: no self-approval/publish option, no confidential data in public fields, and revision history is retained.

### Desk/editor path

1. Decide five pitches: accept, reject, clarify, hold and merge—with valid reasons.
2. Assign an accepted pitch with priority, briefing and future due time.
3. Review a standard story and request revision.
4. Route high-risk content through required fact/legal/source/fairness/right-of-reply checks.
5. Demonstrate that author self-approval and incomplete gates fail.
6. Approve and schedule/publish a corrected fictional story using separate actors.
7. Curate and expire a homepage slot; verify chronological fallback.
8. Run a live event from draft to closed with reporter update, independent approval and correction note.

Pass: every transition, denial and public result matches the workflow matrix.

### Specialist path

Use a fictional article with relevant evidence. Record the specialist’s exact outcome and notes, demonstrate that a failed result blocks approval, then show that the specialist cannot record an unrelated check or publish.

### Multimedia path

Upload a safe test image and an invalid test file. Verify quarantine, complete rights metadata, uploader/approver separation, expiry/restriction behavior, alt text/caption and public derivative rules. Positive production qualification is **blocked** until the selected GD/Imagick pipeline and malware scanner are available.

### Audience/support path

1. Route fictional correction, complaint, privacy and rights messages.
2. Demonstrate the warning that contact is not secure source intake.
3. Request a newsletter subscription, verify hashed-token lifecycle, confirm, unsubscribe and confirm suppression behavior.
4. Explain why no delivery claim is allowed without the reviewed provider/domain.

### Advertising/publisher path

1. Create advertiser and campaign draft as advertising manager.
2. Submit for approval.
3. Attempt creator self-approval (must fail/not appear).
4. Approve with a separate publisher identity.
5. Upload a fictional 600×900 photo, portrait MP4/WebM video or 970×90 header banner with alt text, HTTPS destination, disclosure, rights holder and licence/contract reference; confirm unsafe types, wrong ratios, invalid video containers and missing rights are rejected.
6. Submit the creative; confirm its creator cannot approve it; approve it with the publisher.
7. Create a non-overlapping Right Rail 1–4 or Header Banner 1–4 placement and activate only inside both approved date windows; confirm the right rail appears on homepage/archive/article pages and header banners autoplay with pause controls.
8. Confirm visible disclosure, sponsored-link attributes, automatic placeholder after pause/expiry and audit entries.
9. Generate served/viewable/click test events and explain why privacy-preserving operational counts are not certified invoice evidence.
10. Identify the remaining external contract, scanner, billing-verification and reconciliation evidence.

### Finance path

1. Issue an invoice with unique number/idempotency key.
2. Attempt to record its payment with the same identity (must fail/not appear).
3. Record partial and final payments with a second authorized identity.
4. Attempt duplicate idempotency key and overpayment (must fail).
5. Reconcile the displayed ledger to a fictional external statement manually and document that no production reconciliation integration exists.

### System administrator path

1. Create/suspend a fictional staff account and confirm session invalidation.
2. Assign/remove an allowed secondary role with required reauthentication.
3. Demonstrate that the administrator cannot open editorial content, protected sources or publish.
4. Run local validation commands and inspect redacted evidence.
5. Walk through—not claim completion of—backup restore, incident response and rollback runbooks.

## 4. Cross-role newsroom simulation

Use at least six separate test identities: reporter, editor, specialist, multimedia approver, advertising manager/publisher pair and finance creator/recorder pair.

1. Morning pitch and assignment.
2. Draft with fictional source evidence and media.
3. Review/revision/specialist gates.
4. Independent media and article approval.
5. Scheduled publication and public verification.
6. Correction with visible notice and retained history.
7. Concurrent stale edit/decision attempt and safe rejection.
8. Suspension during an active session and immediate protected-access denial.

Record screenshots/log identifiers only if they contain no secrets or PII.

## 5. Incident tabletop

Facilitate at least these scenarios:

- confidential source copied into a public field;
- compromised editor account during breaking news;
- malicious upload/scanner outage;
- inaccurate live update requiring correction/withdrawal;
- privacy/erasure request and legal-hold conflict;
- duplicate payment reference or ledger discrepancy;
- failed deployment/migration and rollback decision;
- backup restore that cannot decrypt source records.

Pass: learner stops unsafe work, preserves evidence, identifies the correct owner and communicates without disclosing protected data.
