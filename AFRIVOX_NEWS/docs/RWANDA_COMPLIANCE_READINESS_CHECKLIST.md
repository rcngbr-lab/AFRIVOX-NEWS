# Rwanda compliance readiness checklist

Last source review: 2026-10-29. This is an operational checklist, not legal advice and not evidence that AFRIVOX NEWS holds any licence, registration or approval. A qualified Rwandan lawyer/compliance officer must determine applicability and retain official evidence.

| Obligation to confirm | Official source | Responsible owner | Evidence required before launch | Status / review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Register the legal entity and maintain ownership/director/address records | [RDB Office of the Registrar General](https://org.rdb.rw/business-registration/) | Publisher/CEO + Company Secretary | Certificate, registered address, beneficial ownership and current filings | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; verify before contracting |
| Confirm tax/TIN, VAT/EBM and filing obligations for actual revenue/activity | [Rwanda Revenue Authority business registration guidance](https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title) | Finance Manager | TIN, applicable tax registrations, invoicing process and filing calendar | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; finance/legal review |
| Confirm media-organ registration, journalist accreditation, complaints process and applicable media law/code | [Rwanda Media Commission e-services and code/laws](https://rmc.rw/accreditations) | Publisher + Editor-in-Chief + Legal | Media-organ evidence, current code acknowledgement, accredited staff where required, complaints officer | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; RMC confirmation required |
| Confirm whether RURA permit/licensing requirements apply to the selected online, audio-visual or print services | [RURA official portal](https://www.rura.rw/) and [RMC/RURA responsibility memorandum hosted by RGB](https://www.rgb.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RGB/Publications/LAWS_AND_REGULATIONS/MoU-RMC___RURA.pdf) | Legal/Compliance | Written applicability determination and any permit/licence | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; do not infer from software |
| Register as data controller/processor where applicable | [DPO registration provisions](https://dpo.gov.rw/dpp-law/registration-of-a-data-controller-and-a-data-processor) | Data Protection Officer | Registration/certificate, processing inventory, contact point, transfer/risk description | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` |
| Establish lawful, transparent, purpose-limited personal-data processing | [DPO controller/processor obligations](https://dpo.gov.rw/dpp-law/obligations-of-the-data-controller-and-the-data-processor) | DPO + Product + HR | Privacy notices, legal-basis register, retention schedule, processor contracts | Partially supported in software; policy approval required |
| Log access/change/disclosure/erasure of personal data with justification and time | [DPO processing and personal-data logging provisions](https://dpo.gov.rw/dpp-law/processing-and-quality-of-personal-data) | DPO + Security | Source/access/audit reports, access reviews, incident evidence | Application controls implemented; external audit required |
| Implement access, correction, objection, restriction, portability and erasure request handling | [DPO data-subject rights](https://dpo.gov.rw/dpp-law/rights-of-the-data-subject) | DPO + Audience Support + Legal | Request register, identity-verification procedure, decisions and response dates | `PARTIAL`; workflow/policy still required |
| Assess storage or transfers outside Rwanda and obtain required safeguards/authorisation | [DPO transfer/storage guidance](https://dpo.gov.rw/dpp-law/sharing-transferstorage-and-retention-of-personal-data) | DPO + Security + Procurement | Data-flow map, hosting/processor locations, authorisation/safeguards | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; hosting decision required |
| Protect children and vulnerable persons | [DPO FAQ on children and data-subject rights](https://dpo.gov.rw/faqs) | Editor-in-Chief + DPO | Age/consent procedure, editorial safeguarding policy, moderation escalation | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` policy/training |
| Copyright, image/music/video licences and sponsored-content disclosures | RMC code/laws plus applicable IP/contract advice | Legal + Multimedia + Commercial | Licence/contract files, rights metadata, expiry/takedown process and disclosure templates | Media controls implemented; legal taxonomy pending |
| Employment, social-security, workplace safety and freelancer contracts | RDB/RRA/RSSB and labour counsel | HR + Finance | Employment/freelance contracts, payroll/tax/RSSB records, safety training | `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` |
| Physical newspaper permits, printer specifications and distribution contracts | RMC/RURA plus printer/logistics/legal confirmation | Publisher + Print Manager | Demand study, print permit determination, signed printer/distributor SLAs and press-ready spec | `NO-GO` until separate validation |

## Required governance record

For every checklist item, record the current official rule/version, applicability decision, named owner, evidence location, submission/review date, expiry/renewal date and next action. Recheck official sources before launch and at least annually. Software status never substitutes for a licence or professional legal/finance opinion.
