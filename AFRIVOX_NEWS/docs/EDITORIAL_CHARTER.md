# EDITORIAL CHARTER TEMPLATE — adopt only after review

1. **Accuracy.** Obtain primary documents, check time and geography, distinguish verified facts from allegations, and prominently correct mistakes.
2. **Fairness.** Seek relevant response from subjects of critical reporting, quote in context and distinguish objective news from editorials/opinion pieces.
3. **Independence.** Commercial sponsors may not direct factual news coverage. Sponsored work must have prominent labels and commercial disclosure.
4. **Sources.** Document public sources; protect private identities according to specific vetted procedures. Never promise digital anonymity from ordinary email forms.
5. **Photo and video rights.** Require ownership/permission, credit, caption and provenance. Document significant editing. Do not use unrelated photos as purported evidence of an event.
6. **Conflicts.** Require declarations of personal/financial conflicts, recusal and controls on gifts or paid appearances.
7. **Corrections.** Use a visible dated correction note; retain article version history and author/editor audit in production. Provide reader correction channel.
8. **AI.** Humans verify and own accountability for every fact; do not invent quotes, sources, photos or video; document meaningful AI-assisted creation when relevant.
9. **Harm/privacy.** Minimize sensitive personal data, respect children and vulnerable sources, seek expert/legal review for high-risk allegations, and avoid exposing harmful details.
10. **Governance.** Editor-in-chief maintains standards, publishes transparent contact information and reviews appeals, takedown and right-of-reply requests.
