# Financial model, budgets and risk assessment

**Status:** planning model only. Every amount below is an internal scenario assumption in RWF, not a current supplier quote, salary benchmark, customer commitment, tax calculation or forecast assurance. Replace assumptions with verified local quotes, signed contracts and finance/legal review before approval. Taxes, licenses, statutory employment costs and accounting treatment are `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`.

## 1. Model rules

- Use integer minor units in the software; use RWF in management reporting.
- Recognize revenue under an approved accounting policy—not when a prospect expresses interest.
- Track invoiced, collected, deferred, refunded/credited and overdue amounts separately.
- Maintain a rolling 13-week cash forecast and 12-month operating forecast.
- Separate one-time capital/startup costs from recurring operating expense.
- Include tax/statutory/benefit costs only after professional validation.
- Model print as a separate product with printer, delivery and returns unit economics.

## 6. Break-even framework

Use:

```text
Contribution margin = collected revenue – variable delivery/payment/production costs
Contribution margin ratio = contribution margin / collected revenue
Break-even collected revenue = fixed monthly cost / contribution margin ratio
```

Example only: if base fixed/semifixed cost is 20,500,000 RWF and the verified blended contribution margin ratio is 80%, break-even collected revenue is approximately 25,625,000 RWF per month. The 80% ratio is an assumption and must be replaced per revenue stream. Working-capital break-even is later when customers pay slowly.

## 8. Print unit-economics gate

Before any print pilot calculate:

```text
Net copies sold = copies delivered – valid returns – complimentary copies
Net sales = net copies sold × net publisher price
Edition contribution = net sales + edition advertising collected
                       – printing – plates/proofs – transport/distribution
                       – returns handling – editorial/layout incremental cost
Unit contribution = edition contribution / copies produced
```

Proceed only with printer/distributor quotes, confirmed technical specifications, return/cash controls, positive stress-tested contribution and adequate working capital. Otherwise `NO-GO`.

## 9. Finance controls

- Approved chart of accounts, cost centers and revenue recognition.
- Numbered quotations/contracts/invoices with immutable audit history.
- No floating-point money; currency and minor-unit validation.
- Sales cannot mark invoices paid; finance records settlement evidence.
- Refund/credit thresholds require independent authorization.
- Monthly bank/payment-provider reconciliation by a person other than the initiating sales role where feasible.
- Receivable aging, concentration, campaign liability and deferred revenue reviewed monthly.
- Budget-to-actual variance includes cause, owner and action.
- Statutory/tax statements are not produced or represented as compliant without qualified review.

## 10. Financial risk register

| Risk | Exposure | Mitigation / decision trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue ramp is slower than assumed | Runway exhaustion | Hiring gates; 13-week cash forecast; reduce desk/format scope |
| Advertiser concentration | Editorial/commercial pressure and cash shock | Concentration ceiling; diversify; editorial firewall |
| Late/nonpayment | Working-capital crisis | Credit checks/limits, deposits, aging escalation, stop-delivery terms |
| Unfunded investigations | Safety/legal/cash overrun | Assignment budget, stage gates and contingency approval |
| Foreign-currency provider costs | Hosting/software variance | Currency sensitivity and annual-provider review |
| Equipment failure/theft | Production interruption | Asset control, backup kit, insurance/maintenance review |
| Print returns | Unsold inventory and locked cash | No launch without preorder/distribution study |
| Legal/privacy claim | Defense/remediation cost | Policies, counsel, insurance review and documented evidence |
| Fraud/unauthorized refund | Direct loss | Separation, limits, bank reconciliation, immutable audit |
| Tax/employment misclassification | Liability/penalties | Qualified accountant/legal review (`BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`) |

## 11. Funding decision gates

### `CONDITIONAL GO`: digital pilot

- At least six months approved runway after setup and contingency.
- Named finance owner and monthly close/reconciliation process.
- Actual compensation/provider quotes and statutory cost review.
- Audience/sales validation milestones and stop-loss thresholds.
- No dependence on unsigned advertising or print sales.

### `NO-GO`: physical print or permanent studio

Until separate demand, utilization, supplier and unit-economics evidence passes board review.
