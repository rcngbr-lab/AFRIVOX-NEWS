PRIVACY / DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy.

How UBUZIMACARE handles personal information.

Effective: 12 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Scope

The public GitHub Pages edition provides educational pages, local calculators and client-side search. It does not submit calculator inputs, newsletter addresses or contact messages to UBUZIMACARE. Hosting providers may process standard security and access logs under their own terms.

Information collected on the secure production service

If enabled, newsletter registration records the email address, consent time, locale and confirmation status. The contact form records the name, email address, subject, message, status and timestamps. Staff authentication uses secure sessions, account-security events and audit records. Health calculator values are processed in the browser and are not stored by UBUZIMACARE.

Purpose and lawful use

Information is used only to deliver the requested service, protect accounts, answer messages, document consent, maintain editorial accountability and meet applicable legal obligations. It is not sold and health-tool inputs are not used for advertising profiles.

Retention and deletion

Production operators must configure documented retention periods before launch. Newsletter records remain until withdrawal plus the minimum suppression record needed to honour unsubscribe requests. Contact and security records are retained only for the approved operational, legal and security periods, then deleted or anonymised.

Your choices and rights

You may unsubscribe using the message link and may request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Identity verification may be required before fulfilling a request.

Security and processors

The production application uses encrypted transport, role-based access, MFA for privileged roles, protected source records and tamper-evident audit logs. Approved infrastructure, database, email and monitoring providers must be documented before production launch.

Privacy contact

Production status: the legal operator and privacy contact are not yet configured. The production readiness check must fail until both are supplied and reviewed.
