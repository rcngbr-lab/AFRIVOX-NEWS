STATIC PREVIEW CAPABILITIES

What works on this preview

GitHub Pages shows the approved AFRIVOX NEWS visual design. It cannot execute PHP, connect to the newsroom database, authenticate staff, or process private forms.

No result is being simulated. Features below are explicitly unavailable here and become operational only on an approved PHP/MySQL deployment.
PREVIEW ONLY

Articles and categories

Cards display clearly labelled demonstration content for layout review. Individual article and category archives require the production PHP application and eligible CMS records.

PRODUCTION REQUIRED

Search

Search is disabled in this static preview. Production search uses prepared database queries, language filtering, pagination, and returns published eligible articles only.

PRODUCTION REQUIRED

Newsletter

This preview does not collect email addresses. The PHP application provides consent, rate limiting, secure expiring tokens, double opt-in confirmation and unsubscribe processing when an approved mail provider is configured.

PRODUCTION REQUIRED

Language selection

English and Kinyarwanda switching is handled by the PHP application, including editorially approved article translations and explicit fallback behavior. No automatic translation is fabricated here.

PRODUCTION REQUIRED

Video and media

Media-library pages depend on rights-approved CMS assets. The static preview does not claim those workflows are live.

PRODUCTION REQUIRED

RSS and sitemaps

RSS, XML sitemap and News Sitemap are generated by the PHP application from eligible non-demo public content. Demo stories are not published as production news metadata.

INFORMATION

About AFRIVOX NEWS

AFRIVOX NEWS is designed as a digital newsroom serving Rwanda, Africa and global audiences under the tagline “Africa Has a Voice.” Final corporate identity and publication details require organizational approval before launch.

PRODUCTION REQUIRED

Contact and complaints

The production contact form securely records general messages, correction requests and complaints. No contact details or form submission are invented on this preview.

DRAFT

Privacy

The current privacy text is a software framework, not a final legal policy. Controller identity, retention, processors, rights contacts and cross-border handling must be approved before production.

DRAFT

Editorial and fact-checking policy

The platform enforces review, approval, specialist checks, source protection, corrections and retraction controls. Final ownership, deadlines and public commitments require publisher and legal approval.

DRAFT

Corrections

Material errors are corrected transparently, with notices and revision history. Retractions require an explanation and preserve the audit trail.

TARGET

Accessibility

The interface targets WCAG 2.2 AA through semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus, labels and meaningful alternative text. Reported issues must be handled through the production contact process.
